Measured facts

DESK: observed transits

Counts come from deterministic derivation of stored evidence. Unknown means no usable count; partial counts are lower bounds; crossing times are estimates between two observed positions.

DESK: world events

Each case is what its source published, with every correction and retraction kept. Only a sourced report can name an accident, casualty or collision; a lost signal, AIS gap or slowdown is a review candidate and says nothing about the outcome. The reported event location and a track's last observed position are shown separately. A capture shows which reports were published in its reporting window; it cannot show that no event occurred, so whether every event in the view is known stays unknown unless an approved source guarantees it.

Event-report coverage

DESK: news and media evidence

Each item is what a source published: metadata and a link to the original, never a copy of the work. It is evidence that the item was published, not that its account is true. An image or video does not show when or where it was captured, and nothing here is live footage. Items are kept apart from world events; related items are only ever suggestions. Headlines are the sources' own words.
