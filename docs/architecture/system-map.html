EYE, laid out

This is the whole public EYE codebase as one map. Data moves left to right: synthetic sources are captured, stored as evidence in PostgreSQL, turned into tracks and counts by workers, and served through a bounded API to the THEATRE and DESK views.

  • Scroll or pinch to zoom. Drag the background to pan.
  • Click any block to see what it does, what feeds it and what it feeds.
  • Ask Claude about anything on the map, or switch to Redesign to change it.
